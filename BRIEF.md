# Scrollcraft Brief — ServiceNow Capability Atlas

## Source of direction

- User interview answers (verbatim): “Warm and pleasant mood with AI and technology theme, visitors should get important starting point of servicenow capabilities and follow as expansion of each capabilities with more detailed text and expansion details. Page should feel intense, aesthetics should be playful an dense with distinct scenes/chapters. Use existing graphics as well as new graphics also and use generative imagery as well”
- Other user direction: the site should be coherent, easy to navigate, show ServiceNow capabilities and use data to derive interactive maps and clickable flow charts. It should not read like a presentation browser.
- User supplied no named visual references or exact memorable moment. The experience and peak below are authored design decisions.

## Visitor and outcome

- **Subject:** a connected learning guide to ServiceNow capabilities, workflows, platform foundations and current product context.
- **Visitor:** a learner or practitioner who wants to move from the broad platform picture into practical capability detail.
- **Belief to install:** capabilities become easier to understand when viewed as connected work on shared AI, data and workflow foundations.
- **Next action:** follow a work journey, then open a capability profile or official product guidance.

## Journey and feeling

| Beat | Feeling | Experience |
|---|---|---|
| Orient | Warm, clear | Type-first title page sets the platform foundations and gives direct chapter links. |
| Locate | Curious | Select a work domain; the map and detail panel expose its connected capabilities. |
| Trace | Energized | Follow a request from signal through context, coordination, AI assistance and improvement. |
| Understand | Confident | Open a capability to see its outcome, takeaways, relationships, visuals and official context. |
| Continue | In control | Filter capabilities or follow current product updates to continue learning. |

- **Peak:** selecting a capability reveals the connected route from work domain through platform services to outcomes, and lets the visitor follow that route into another capability.
- **Tell-someone sentence:** “I can start with a business need, follow the work, and see which ServiceNow capabilities connect to the outcome.”
- **Authored pause:** a short quiet beat between the broad ecosystem view and the detailed journey; no extra cue or motion is added there.

## Grammar, navigation, signature

- **Grammar:** Chaptered Editorial. The visitor explicitly asked for distinct chapters and a detailed learning progression. Keep real search, filters, map selection and workflow controls available within that reading structure.
- **Navigation:** an in-flow chapter folio near the opening with direct links and clear chapter numbers; no fixed sidebar or sticky application bar.
- **Title page:** warm paper ground and typography first, with no media above the fold.
- **Closing pattern:** a functional capability search / official-source route, not a promotional CTA.
- **Signature move:** capability rethreading. Selecting a capability brings its work domain, platform foundations, related capabilities and outcome into one connected path, with related capability chips continuing that path.

## Art direction

- Warm cream paper, coral, persimmon, teal, golden yellow and deep ink; playful annotation and dense but legible editorial hierarchy.
- Use existing product screenshots and diagrams where they explain a capability; use one cohesive generated editorial illustration to make the connected-work idea tangible.
- Avoid generic stock photography, futuristic blue neon, full-bleed marketing imagery and deck-navigation language.

## Scroll score

| Chapter | Device | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Orient | `flow` + `in` | Let visitors scan the type-first opening and follow direct chapter links. |
| Locate | `flow` + `reveal` | Reveal the interactive ecosystem and its domain relationships. |
| Trace | `flow` + `parallax` | Give the chapter illustration slight depth beside the actual interactive workflow. |
| Understand | `flow` + `in` + `count` | Bring the capability index into focus and animate only genuine portfolio totals. |
| Continue | `flow` + `reveal` | End on current documentation and an easy route back into exploration. |

No video scrub, full-screen footage, pinned crossfade text or fixed bar is used. Scroll advances distinct chapters; direct interaction remains available.
